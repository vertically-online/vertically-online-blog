## What a roofing answering service should actually do

A roofing answering service isn't there to say "please hold." It's there to stop a homeowner from calling the next roofer while you're on a job. That gives you four questions to judge every option by:

1. **How fast does a real response happen?** Day, night, and weekend.
2. **Does it book the inspection, or just take a message?** A message still needs you to call back.
3. **Does it handle web leads, or only phone calls?** Many roofing leads arrive through a website form.
4. **What does it cost when you're busy?** Per-minute and per-call overages add up during storm season.

Research published in the Harvard Business Review found that [most companies are not responding to online leads nearly fast enough](https://hbr.org/2011/03/the-short-life-of-online-sales-leads). Roofing is no different. Speed is the reason this category exists.

## Human answering services vs. AI receptionists

**Human answering services** put a live receptionist on your line. They're good at empathy and at handling odd calls. They're usually priced by the minute or by the call, and booking appointments may be included or cost extra.

**AI receptionists** answer instantly, don't run out of minutes in the same way, and can book directly onto a calendar. Quality varies a lot, so test before you commit.

Neither is automatically better. The right choice depends on whether your bottleneck is answering the phone, or turning the call into a booked inspection.

### PATLive

PATLive is a human answering service. Its pricing page states that it ["does not use AI to answer calls"](https://www.patlive.com/pricing/) and that 24/7 answering and appointment scheduling are included in every plan. Plans are priced by minutes, with per-minute overage rates.

### Ruby

Ruby offers live virtual receptionists around the clock, with [plans priced by included minutes](https://www.ruby.com/pricing/) and an optional live chat add-on. Appointment scheduling is listed among the receptionist plan features.

### Smith.ai

Smith.ai offers [AI-first, hybrid, and human-first](https://smith.ai/pricing) answering. Its published plans include lead screening and 24/7 live staffing; appointment booking is a per-call add-on.

**Best for:** roofers who want a human safety net with intake and CRM integration.
**Watch for:** add-ons (booking, recording, Spanish line) are charged per call.

### AnswerConnect

AnswerConnect emphasizes ["Always people, not bots"](https://www.answerconnect.com/) with 24/7 coverage including weekends and holidays, and it integrates with scheduling tools. Its homepage says you choose a monthly plan based on minutes; prices aren't listed there.

**Best for:** roofers who specifically want humans only.
**Watch for:** request pricing before you compare.

### Rosie

Rosie is an [AI answering service](https://heyrosie.com/pricing) with minute-based plans. On its higher plan it books appointments directly onto your calendar; the entry plan texts callers a booking link instead.

**Best for:** a low-cost AI answering line you set up yourself.
**Watch for:** it's general-purpose, so roofing-specific intake and follow-up are up to you to configure.

### Goodcall

Goodcall is an [AI phone agent](https://www.goodcall.com/pricing) priced per unique customer rather than per minute, so repeat callers don't add cost the same way.

**Best for:** roofers who want to build and test an AI agent themselves before paying.
**Watch for:** check how it books into your calendar; the pricing page doesn't describe it.

## How to choose

Run this test with every finalist. Call it at 7 PM on a weekday, as a homeowner with a leak. Then fill out your own website form. Measure:

- **Seconds until a real response** on both channels.
- **Whether you end up with a booked inspection**, or just a promise of a callback.
- **What lands in your inbox** and whether you could act on it without calling back blind.
- **What the bill would be in your busiest month**, including overages.
