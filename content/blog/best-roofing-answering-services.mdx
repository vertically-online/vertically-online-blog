## What makes a good roofer answering service

Four things matter more than anything else:

1. **Speed at every hour.** Day, night, weekend, and during a storm surge, when every roofer's phone rings at once.
2. **An inspection, not a message.** A message still needs you to call back, and by then the homeowner may have booked someone else.
3. **Web leads as well as calls.** Plenty of roofing leads come through a website form, not the phone.
4. **A bill you can predict.** Per-minute and per-call pricing climbs fastest in your busiest weeks.

If you want a deeper checklist, see [what to look for in an answering service before you sign](/blog/what-to-look-for-in-a-roofing-answering-service).

### Vertically (our product)

Vertically is built only for roofing companies, but it isn't a phone answering service: **it doesn't answer inbound calls.** It handles the leads that come in through your website. Each quote request gets an instant estimate by email from your own price list (bigger jobs come to you first) and a text. About 5 minutes later, an AI agent calls the homeowner and can book the inspection on your Google Calendar. Homeowners who go quiet get follow-up, and finished jobs get a review request. We set it up for you.

**Suits:** roofers who lose web leads to slow replies. Pair it with a phone answering service if you also miss a lot of calls.
**Watch for:** pricing after the free 30 days isn't published; it's agreed after a review of your results. [See how Vertically compares with a roofing answering service](/roofing-answering-service).

### PinkCallers

PinkCallers [pairs a dedicated human receptionist with AI overflow](https://pinkcallers.com/) and has roofing-specific pages. It's a ServiceTitan Certified Call Center and supports Housecall Pro. Its published rates are hourly for daytime desks, weekly for after-hours coverage, with per-booking and per-dispatch fees.

**Suits:** roofing companies that want a person owning their phones during the day and run ServiceTitan or Housecall Pro.
**Watch for:** contract terms aren't stated on the homepage, so ask before you commit.

### Direct Line Answers

Direct Line Answers is a [human answering service](https://www.directlineanswers.com/) with 24/7/365 coverage, urgent dispatch, and escalation. Roofers are one of many industries it lists. Plans start at $115 a month.

**Suits:** roofers who want live agents and a low published starting price.
**Watch for:** the homepage doesn't describe appointment booking, so confirm how leads reach you.

### AnswerMTI

AnswerMTI is a [24×7 live telephone answering service](https://www.answermti.com/) with appointment scheduling and a roofing page. It offers a free 7-day trial with no credit card and month-to-month agreements.

**Suits:** roofers who want people on the phone around the clock, without a long contract.
**Watch for:** prices aren't on the homepage; check its pricing page.

### OnCrew

OnCrew is an [AI phone answering service for trades](https://oncrew.ai/), configured for HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and roofing contractors, with 24/7 answering. It captures appointment requests, emails summaries, and puts requests on your Google Calendar when connected.

**Suits:** small roofing crews who want an inexpensive, roofing-aware AI line.
**Watch for:** per-call pricing over your plan's included calls.

### Upfirst

Upfirst is an [AI answering service](https://upfirst.ai/) that answers 24/7 and books into Google Calendar, Outlook, Calendly, and other tools. Contractors are a listed industry, though roofing isn't named.

**Suits:** roofers who want the lowest published starting price and simple calendar booking.
**Watch for:** the entry plan covers 30 calls a month.

### Dialzara

Dialzara is an [AI receptionist for small businesses](https://dialzara.com/) that answers around the clock and books appointments to your calendar. It isn't roofing-specific.

**Suits:** roofers comfortable configuring a general AI receptionist themselves.
**Watch for:** voice, SMS, and outbound agents are priced as separate products.

### LeadTruffle

LeadTruffle is an [AI lead-response platform for home services](https://leadtruffle.co/) that pulls leads from paid sources, your website, and your phone into one inbox, with AI text, email, and voice. It books when configured.

**Suits:** roofers buying leads from several sources who want them in one place.
**Watch for:** pricing is through sales; there's no published price or trial.

## How to choose

Call each finalist at 7 PM as a homeowner with a leak, then fill out your own website form. Measure the seconds until a real response, whether you end up with a booked inspection, what lands in your inbox, and what the bill would be in your busiest month.

<Recommendation>
If you want a person on every call, start with a human or hybrid service like **AnswerMTI** or **PinkCallers** and set strict callback rules for your team.
