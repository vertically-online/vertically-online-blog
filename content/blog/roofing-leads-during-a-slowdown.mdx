[U.S. shingle shipments fell 13% in the third quarter of 2026](https://www.roofingcontractor.com/articles/102831-us-shingle-shipments-fall-13-in-third-quarter), and that is a useful warning for anyone tracking roofing leads during a slowdown. The Roofing Contractor report on Asphalt Roofing Manufacturers Association data puts shipments at 35,156,884 squares, down from 40,387,814 a year earlier. This week, check how fast your current requests get a real reply, then compare your own lead counts month over month before you change your marketing.

## What the Q3 Shingle Drop Means for Your Lead Flow

### Residential demand is the pressure point

The report ties the drop to residential pressure, with [builder confidence at a 14-year low in October](https://www.roofingcontractor.com/articles/102831-us-shingle-shipments-fall-13-in-third-quarter). Shipment data is a manufacturer measure, so treat it as a signal about the market, not a forecast for your territory.

### Why national numbers still need a local check

National shipment data cannot show what is happening in your ZIP codes. Pull your own lead counts for the last six months, sort them by service type, and look at the trend. That shows whether residential requests are slipping while other work holds steady.

## Roofing Leads During a Slowdown: What to Do First

### Stop losing the leads you already have

When demand softens, each lead matters more, so audit how long requests sit before anyone replies. Check your phone, email and web inbox together, since many requests arrive outside business hours and wait until morning. A request left for a full day is one a competitor may answer first. For the wider process, read [how to get more roofing leads without losing the ones you have](/blog/how-to-get-more-roofing-leads).

### Speed up quoting on residential requests

[BofA Securities said weak storm activity weighed on end-market demand, according to a report on Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/analyst-ratings/bofa-cuts-owens-corning-stock-price-target-on-weak-shingle-demand-93CH-4974483). Plan for fewer urgent, storm-driven calls and more homeowners comparing options before they sign. A faster first response and a clear written quote keep you in the running while they shop.

## Where the Demand Is Holding Up

### Low-slope and commercial work

[Modified bitumen shipments rose 17% year over year in the third quarter](https://www.roofingcontractor.com/articles/102831-us-shingle-shipments-fall-13-in-third-quarter) while shingle shipments fell. The same report frames the low-slope gain as steadier commercial opportunity. Shipments are not job counts, so use this as a reason to test, not a guarantee.

Consider a short outreach list of commercial property owners and property managers in your service area. Send a brief note that describes your low-slope work and asks who handles roof repairs for their buildings.

### Reading the mix of product categories

Track which job types are still booking before you change your marketing spend. A category that holds steady on your own calendar matters more than any national trend line.

## Costs and Labor Pressure Still Matter

### Why the slump does not mean cheaper jobs

A [2026 market update from the Ilroofinginstitute blog](https://www.ilroofinginstitute.com/blog/us-roofing-market-in-slump) describes the U.S. roofing market as in a slump, while tariffs, a shortage of skilled roofers and workforce raids squeeze contractors. Slower demand does not mean your costs fell. [BofA Securities said cost inflation remains elevated in asphalt and transportation.](https://www.investing.com/news/analyst-ratings/bofa-cuts-owens-corning-stock-price-target-on-weak-shingle-demand-93CH-4974483)

Review your price list at least quarterly so quotes reflect current material and labor costs. A quote built on last year's numbers can cost you either the job or your margin.

## A Practical Week of Lead Follow-Up

### Simple steps for the next seven days

1. Pull last month's leads and note how many got a reply within one business day.
2. Set a standing follow-up schedule for quotes that have not booked an inspection, and write the messages now so they are ready to send.
3. Ask customers who were happy with the work for a Google review, and tag which leads came from search so you know what to repeat.
