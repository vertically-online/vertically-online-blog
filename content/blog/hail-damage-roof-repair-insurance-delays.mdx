Delays can allow more water damage, which can enlarge the scope of work.

## Why owners wait on insurance and what roofers can control

Property owners often cannot start a repair until the adjuster inspects the damage and the insurer approves payment. That wait is outside your control. What you can control is how quickly you are ready once the money clears.

### Where the delay happens

The holdup usually sits between the owner's claim and the insurer's decision, not with the roofer. You will rarely know the adjuster's schedule or the claim status, so plan around uncertainty rather than trying to predict it.

### What you can do while owners wait

- **Document the damage with dated photos** from the roof and interior, and note what you saw and when.
- **Write a scope in plain terms** so the owner can share it with the adjuster and you can move fast when approval arrives.
- **Don't promise claim outcomes.** Whether the insurer pays, and how much, is not yours to say. Stick to what you can inspect and quote.

## A checklist for hail damage roof repair leads this week

1. **Contact commercial owners and property managers you already know** who had recent hail. Offer a temporary tarp or water mitigation option while they wait on funds.
