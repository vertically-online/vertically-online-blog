**Disclosure:** Vertically publishes this guide and makes one of the products below. Everyone else's details come from their own websites, linked, as of **October 21, 2026**. Prices and plans change often, so confirm on each vendor's site.

## Three different jobs hiding under one name

"Roofing estimating software" and "roofing proposal software" cover three different jobs. Knowing which one you're hiring for saves you a lot of demos.

1. **Measure and estimate.** Get roof measurements, turn them into materials and a price, and produce a report. This is the job for estimators and sales reps.
2. **Run the sales process.** Track leads and jobs, send proposals for e-signature, order materials, and manage crews. This is a CRM.
3. **Quote the homeowner instantly.** Give a price on your website the moment someone asks, before anyone drives out. This is where most jobs are won or lost, because it's when the homeowner decides who to call back.

Most tools below focus on one or two of these. None of them does all three equally well.

### Vertically

Vertically handles the first quote automatically. A homeowner answers a few questions on your website and gets a PDF estimate built from **your own price list** in under a minute, sent from your email under your company name. Jobs outside the limits you set are held for your review. Then the system books the inspection on your Google Calendar with an automatic call, and follows up with anyone who goes quiet.

**Best for:** roofers losing jobs because quoting takes days and leads go cold.
**Not built for:** detailed measured proposals after the inspection. Pair it with a field tool if your reps need one. [See how the Vertically roofing quote software works](/roofing-quote-software).

### Roofr

Roofr combines a CRM, proposals with e-signatures, and measurement reports, with an [Instant Estimator available as an add-on](https://roofr.com/pricing). Its free Starter plan includes 10 trial proposals; paid plans add unlimited proposals and e-signatures, and measurement reports are priced per report.

**Best for:** small to mid-size roofers who want proposals, measurements, and a job board in one place.
**Watch for:** the instant estimator and some other products are add-ons, so price the full bundle you'd use.

### Roofle RoofQuote PRO

Roofle's RoofQuote PRO is an [instant online roof quote tool](https://offers.roofle.com/) for contractor websites. Roofle describes "accurate 30 second multi-structure roof quotes," with digital proposals, e-sign contracts, and products and pricing the contractor customizes. It also offers homeowner financing pre-qualification.

**Best for:** roofers who want a self-serve quote widget and financing on their website.
**Watch for:** it's a quote tool, so responding to, following up with, and booking the lead is still on you.

### AccuLynx

AccuLynx is a [roofing CRM and business management platform](https://www.acculynx.com/) with estimating that pulls in measurement data and live material pricing, custom sales proposals, 20+ integrations including aerial measurements such as EagleView, and direct ordering with suppliers including ABC Supply, QXO, and SRS.

**Best for:** established roofing companies running a full sales and production process.
**Watch for:** pricing is quote-based, and it doesn't measure roofs itself; it integrates with measurement providers.

### JobNimbus

JobNimbus is a contractor CRM whose [plans all include unlimited estimates, documents, and eSign](https://www.jobnimbus.com/pricing), plus invoices, financing, supplier integrations, and payments. Its SumoQuote product handles proposals separately.

**Best for:** teams that want a flexible CRM with estimates and e-signatures built in.
**Watch for:** pricing is on request, and plan tiers cap users, boards, and automations.

### RoofSnap

RoofSnap focuses on [measurements and estimates](https://roofsnap.com/pricing/), with pay-as-you-go measurement orders or per-user subscriptions. Its plans include good-better-best estimates and payments; subscriptions add branded reports and contracts, material ordering, and financing.

**Best for:** estimators who measure and price every roof.
**Watch for:** report prices vary by type and roof size; check the chart for the reports you'd order most.

## How to choose

Start with where your jobs are getting stuck:

- **Leads go cold before you quote them?** You need the quote to happen instantly, and someone to follow up. Look at instant-quote options, and make sure something also books the inspection.
- **Quotes take your reps too long after the inspection?** Look at measurement and estimating tools like RoofSnap or Roofr.
- **Jobs fall through the cracks between sales and production?** Look at a CRM like AccuLynx or JobNimbus.

Then test with a real job. Time how long it takes from "homeowner asks for a price" to "price in the homeowner's inbox," and from there to "inspection on the calendar."

## Questions to ask in every demo

Sales demos are built to show the best path through the product. Ask these to see the path you'll actually use:

- **Where do the prices come from?** Your own price list and markups, an industry database, or a mix? Can you set different pricing by material, pitch, or job size?
- **What happens to the jobs you don't want to quote automatically?** Can you set limits, and does anything outside them come to you for review first?
- **Who does the setup?** Building price books and templates takes real time. Find out whether the vendor does it or you do.
- **How does the quote reach the homeowner?** From your email address and under your brand, or from the vendor's?
- **What happens after the quote?** Does anything follow up, or book the inspection, or does the lead sit until someone on your team picks it up?
- **What will it cost in your busiest month?** Count per-report, per-user, and add-on fees, not just the base plan.
