A Farming Simulator 25 mod called Roofing Insurance simulates storm claims on farm buildings. It is a game, not a real insurance rule change, so nothing about it changes how policies or carriers work. The useful takeaway for a roofing business is to check your own storm-lead process: how you record damage, how you present repair options, and how quickly you follow up.

## What roofers should do this week

Pull up the last five storm-related jobs you handled and check whether each one has dated photos of the damage, a written note of the location and damage type, and a record of which repair option the homeowner chose. If any are missing, fix that process before the next storm season. Your roofing insurance claim workflow for contractors is only as strong as the records behind it.

## What the mod shows about repair choices

The mod, described by its publisher as a [first-release storm insurance mod for FS25](https://farmingsimulator25mods.com/roofing-insurance-v1-0), lets a player choose between an insurer-arranged rebuild and a cash repair. Its listing also logs each storm-hit building as a damage site with a severity and value. Those mechanics are game design. They do not tell you how any real carrier assesses, depreciates, or pays for hail damage, so do not apply them to a real claim.

## Documentation habits that hold up

For every storm-hit job, photograph and date each damaged area before repairs begin, and record the address and building. Keep a simple damage log with location, damage type and notes. Write down which repair option the homeowner selected and why, without promising any coverage outcome. Those records protect you and the homeowner, whatever the insurer decides.

## Moving storm leads through your pipeline

Homeowners after a storm often have urgent questions. A fast, clear first response keeps them from calling the next roofer. Set a written follow-up schedule for leads waiting on an insurance decision, so they do not go cold. Keep the quote and any inspection in one place so the next step is clear to you and the customer. ## Keeping leads warm while a claim is pending

Tell the homeowner what you can do now, such as an inspection or temporary protection, while the claim is open. A regular check-in helps them know you are still available. Tag leads that came from storm events so you can see how that work performs over time.
