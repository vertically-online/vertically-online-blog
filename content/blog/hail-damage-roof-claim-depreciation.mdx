## What to do this week when a hail claim lands on your desk

### Check the homeowner's policy type before quoting

Ask the homeowner for the declarations page and find out whether the policy pays replacement cost or actual cash value. Homeowners policies settle claims in one of two ways: replacement cost pays what a new roof costs today minus the deductible, while actual cash value subtracts depreciation ([Smart Insurance 101](https://smartinsurance101.com/homeowners-insurance-roof-damage-and-replacement-coverage/)). Look for a roof payment schedule and any separate wind or hail deductible too, since those change the check.

### Document the roof age and condition

Take dated photos of every slope, the shingle condition, and any prior damage before you write the scope. Adjusters use roof age to calculate depreciation, so your records let you test their math. Roof age is also an argument insurers make: one source notes that an adjuster may read hail damage on a very old asphalt roof as partly aging rather than the storm ([Elevated Roofing](https://elevatedroofing.com/news-tips/how-often-will-insurance-pay/)).

## Why the adjuster's number can be far below your bill

### Replacement cost versus actual cash value

The gap usually comes from the payout method, not a dispute over the work. The same news story describes an offer far below the contractor's estimate even though the adjuster's math was technically consistent ([24/7 Wall St.Under actual cash value, the homeowner receives the replacement cost less depreciation, so the same roof can produce two very different checks.

### Age-based depreciation on older roofs

Age-based depreciation can cut a payout by up to half on roofs over 20 years old ([Smart Insurance 101](https://smartinsurance101.com/homeowners-insurance-roof-damage-and-replacement-coverage/)). Some insurers also switch from replacement cost to actual cash value as roofs age ([Dalyandalexander](https://dalyandalexander.com/roof-replacement-coverage/)). Before you assume the adjuster erred, ask at what roof age that carrier changes the terms.

## How to present a roof replacement quote to a homeowner with a claim

### Separate covered damage from age-related wear

Split your scope into two groups: hail damage from the storm, and existing wear. Insurance responds to sudden events, not the slow passage of time, so shingles that curled or cracked from age are usually not covered ([Dalyandalexander](https://dalyandalexander.com/roof-replacement-coverage/)). A covered loss may also repair only the damaged part of the roof rather than the whole thing ([Dalyandalexander](https://dalyandalexander.com/roof-replacement-coverage/)). Writing the scope this way shows the homeowner which items the policy is likely to pay for.

### Explain the deductible and possible percentage wind or hail deductible

Your policy has a deductible, usually $500 to $2,500, and that amount comes off the payout ([Housing Info](https://housinginfo.org/roofing/does-insurance-cover-roof-replacement)). Some states apply a separate wind or hail deductible as a percentage of dwelling coverage ([Smart Insurance 101](https://smartinsurance101.com/homeowners-insurance-roof-damage-and-replacement-coverage/)). Tell the homeowner that the deductible is set by their policy and that you cannot predict the final check.
