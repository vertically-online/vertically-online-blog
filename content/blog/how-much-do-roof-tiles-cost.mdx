Customers asking how much do roof tiles cost are usually comparing your number against a price they found online. Published ranges are wide, and they mix units, currencies and scope, so a quote that looks high may just be built differently. This post shows the published ranges so you can check your own tile pricing and tighten the quotes homeowners compare.

## What to Do This Week About Roof Tile Prices

Pull your current supplier costs for each tile type you install. Compare them against the UK per-tile and per-square-metre ranges from [North West Roofing Supplies' tile cost guide](https://northwestroofingsupplies.co.uk/how-much-do-roof-tiles-cost-on-average/), and check whether your per-square figures match the material cost you are passing on.

Then remember that tiles are only part of the total. The same supplier's [re-roof cost guide](https://northwestroofingsupplies.co.uk/cost-of-replacing-a-roof-with-new-tiles/) says labour can be 50 to 60% of a full re-roof, so a tile quote that shows only material cost will look misleading to a homeowner.

## How Much Do Roof Tiles Cost by Material

### Concrete tiles

In the UK, [standard concrete interlocking tiles run about £0.80 to £2.00 per tile](https://northwestroofingsupplies.co.uk/how-much-do-roof-tiles-cost-on-average/), or roughly £20 to £40 per square metre for materials. Tile prices across types generally run £30 to £100+ per square metre, according to the [re-roof cost guide](https://northwestroofingsupplies.co.uk/cost-of-replacing-a-roof-with-new-tiles/).

### Clay and premium tiles

Machine-made clay interlocking tiles cost about £40 to £80 per square metre in North West Roofing Supplies' [tile cost guide](https://northwestroofingsupplies.co.uk/how-much-do-roof-tiles-cost-on-average/), and handmade or heritage clay can go well above that. Natural slate spans a wide range, with Spanish and Brazilian options at the lower end and Welsh slate at the top.

## Why Quotes for the Same Job Vary So Much

Roof size, tile profile, underlay, battens, fixings and access all change the total, as the UK supplier notes in its [re-roof guide](https://northwestroofingsupplies.co.uk/cost-of-replacing-a-roof-with-new-tiles/). Scaffolding alone can add hundreds of pounds. A barrel or S-profile tile can cost more to install than a simple interlocking one.

Different sources also use different units and currencies. Check whether a figure is per tile, per square metre or per square foot, and whether it includes labour, before you compare it with your own number.

## How to Build a Tile Quote Customers Can Trust

Itemise tiles, underlay, battens, fixings, accessories and labour so the homeowner can see what drives the price. A quote that shows the price of a clay tile next to the cost of ridge units and scaffolding is easier to defend than one line that says "re-roof."

Set a clear review point for very large jobs instead of guessing a number. If a job is above the size or value you are comfortable quoting from a standard price list, say so and arrange a site visit.
