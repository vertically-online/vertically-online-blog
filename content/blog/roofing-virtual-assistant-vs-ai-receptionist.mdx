If you run a small roofing company, you've probably considered getting help with the phones and the office. The two most common options are a **roofing virtual assistant**, a remote person who works for you part-time or full-time, and an **AI receptionist**, software that answers leads automatically.

They're often compared as if they do the same job. They don't. This guide explains what each one really does, how they're priced, and when each is the better choice, including when our own product, Vertically, isn't the right fit.

**Disclosure:** Vertically, which publishes this guide, makes an AI answering system for roofing companies.

## What a roofing virtual assistant does

A virtual assistant is a person, usually remote, who takes work off your plate. For a roofing company, that typically means:

- **Answering calls** during the hours they work.
- **Scheduling** inspections and jobs on your calendar.
- **Following up** with leads and customers.
- **Admin:** updating your CRM, chasing signatures, sending invoices, organising photos and paperwork.
- **Coordinating** with homeowners, crews, and sometimes suppliers.

The big strength of a good assistant is judgment. They can handle an upset homeowner, sort out a scheduling mess, or chase the paperwork on an insurance claim. The limit is time: they work set hours, they need training, and they can only be in one conversation at once.

## What an AI receptionist does

An AI receptionist is software that answers your leads automatically. For roofing, the useful ones:

- **Answer instantly, at any hour,** including nights, weekends, and storm surges when every roofer's phone rings at once.
- **Capture the job details:** address, type of work, urgency.
- **Book the inspection** straight onto your calendar.
- **Follow up** with homeowners who go quiet.

Some go further. Vertically, for example, also replies to web leads by text and email in under 30 seconds, sends quotes from your own price list (bigger jobs come to you first), and asks for reviews after the job. See [how an AI roofing answering service works](/roofing-answering-service).

The limit is scope. An AI receptionist is built for leads. It won't sort out your supplier order, file insurance paperwork, or calm down a frustrated customer the way an experienced person can.

## How the costs really compare

It's tempting to compare an hourly rate with a monthly software plan, but that misses how leads arrive. Roofing leads come in when homeowners are home: evenings, weekends, and right after storms. To cover those hours with a person, you're paying for a lot of hours, many of them quiet.

A fairer comparison is: **what does it cost to have every lead answered within a minute, at every hour leads arrive?** Work that out with your own lead volume and the quotes you get. For the lead-response part of the job, AI usually covers more hours for the money. For everything else in the office, it can't help, and a person can.

## When a virtual assistant is the better choice

Choose a person if:

- **Your bottleneck is admin, not speed.** You answer leads fine, but paperwork, invoicing, and scheduling crews eat your evenings.
- **You do a lot of insurance work.** Claims mean documents, adjuster calls, and follow-up that need human judgment.
- **Your customers need a lot of hand-holding.** Complex commercial jobs or long sales cycles benefit from one person who knows every detail.
- **You want one person doing many different things.** An assistant can switch between phones, CRM, social media, and paperwork.

In these cases Vertically isn't the right fit on its own. It handles leads, not your back office.

## When an AI receptionist is the better choice

Choose AI if:

- **You lose jobs because nobody gets back fast enough.** A homeowner who waits a few hours often books someone else.
- **Your leads arrive after hours.** Evenings, weekends, and storm weeks are when you most need an answer and least have one.
- **Quoting and booking eat your time.** Every lead turns into back-and-forth before anything gets scheduled.
- **You want the same process every time,** without training or turnover.

## The setup many small roofers end up with

You don't have to pick one forever. A common pattern:

1. **AI handles every new lead:** instant reply, details captured, quote within your limits, inspection booked.
2. **A person handles the office:** paperwork, invoicing, insurance follow-up, and the customer conversations that need judgment.

That way your assistant's hours go to work only a person can do, and no lead waits for someone to be free.
