Roof tile cost per square metre varies far more than most quick searches suggest. Published UK figures put concrete interlocking tiles at £0.80 to £2.00 per tile, and tile prices generally at £30 to £100 or more per square metre depending on type ([North West Roofing Supplies](https://northwestroofingsupplies.co.uk/cost-of-replacing-a-roof-with-new-tiles/)). For a roofer, those numbers are a sanity check, not a price. Your quote has to come from your own material, labour and markup rates.

## What the published tile cost ranges actually show

### Per-tile and per-square-metre figures

Per-tile pricing is the easiest number to find. One supplier cites standard concrete interlocking tiles at £0.80 to £2.00 each, and roughly £20 to £40 per square metre for materials only. Machine-made clay interlocking tiles come in higher, at about £40 to £80 per square metre, and heritage or handmade clay can go well past £150 ([North West Roofing Supplies](https://northwestroofingsupplies.co.uk/how-much-do-roof-tiles-cost-on-average/)).

### Why the range is so wide

The same supplier notes that material type, manufacturer, profile and order quantity all move the price. Welsh slate, for example, is cited at £80 to £150 or more per square metre, while Spanish slate sits far lower ([North West Roofing Supplies](https://northwestroofingsupplies.co.uk/how-much-do-roof-tiles-cost-on-average/)). A single average for "tiles" hides most of what drives a quote.

## Why a roof tile cost per square metre figure is not your quote

### Materials versus labour

A supplier's guide to re-roofing a typical three-bedroom semi-detached house, around 50 to 60 square metres of roof, puts the total at £5,000 to £12,000 or more. It also says labour can make up 50 to 60 percent of that total ([North West Roofing Supplies](https://northwestroofingsupplies.co.uk/cost-of-replacing-a-roof-with-new-tiles/)). If your quote only prices tiles, it will look cheap and then lose money on the roof.

### Accessories and underlay add cost

The same guide lists breathable underlay at £3 to £8 per square metre, timber battens at £2 to £5 per metre, plus fixings, flashing and scaffolding, which it describes as almost always needed ([North West Roofing Supplies](https://northwestroofingsupplies.co.uk/cost-of-replacing-a-roof-with-new-tiles/)). None of these sit inside the tile price.

Before you compare any published figure with your own work, check which lines your quotes include. A tile-only number is not comparable to a full re-roof number.

## How to use a roof tile cost per square metre check on your own quotes

Build each estimate from your own price list. Set your material costs, crew day rate, scaffolding cost and markup so the quote reflects what the job costs you, not an industry average.

Use published ranges only as a check. If your per-square-metre figure falls far outside the usual band for that tile type, look for a missing line item or a pricing error before you send it.

Send unusual jobs to a site visit rather than an instant figure. Complex roofs, heritage tiles, many penetrations and large areas are where averages mislead most.
