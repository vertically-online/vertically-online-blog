## Why roofers search for an answering service

Most roofers looking for a roofing answering service have the same problem: leads come in while they're on a roof, and by the time they get back to them, the homeowner has booked someone else. An answering service is one fix for the calls. It does nothing for the quote requests sitting in your website inbox.

There are three kinds of phone answering service:

- **Human answering services** put a live receptionist on your line. They're good with unusual calls and upset customers. They usually take a message or follow a script, and they're typically billed by the minute or by the call.
- **AI answering services** pick up your line with a voice agent. The better ones capture job details and book onto your calendar.
- **Hybrid services** pair people with AI, for example a human during business hours and AI overflow at night.

**Vertically is none of these.** It doesn't answer inbound calls. It makes sure the leads that come in through your website never go cold.

## Why web leads go cold

A homeowner fills out your form at 2 PM; you see it at 8 PM and call back the next morning. By then they've often booked whoever got back to them first. You didn't lose that job on price or workmanship. You lost it on time.

Slow replies also cost you hours. Every lead turns into calls, texts, and emails to gather details, price the job, and send a quote, usually in the evening after a full day on a roof.

Arias Roofing & Siding in Sewell, NJ lived this. They were getting 1 to 3 leads a month, mostly referrals, and replies took 24 to 48 hours. After installing Vertically, with no new ad spend, replies dropped to under 30 seconds and jobs went from about one a month to about five. The full numbers are below.

## What Vertically does with every web lead

1. **A homeowner requests a quote.** Your quote page asks the questions that fit your services: type of work, size, materials, or a guided flow if they don't know.
2. **An instant estimate by email.** Priced from your own labor rates, costs, materials and markups, sent as a branded PDF from your own email address.
3. **Big jobs come to you.** If a job is above the ceiling you set, the homeowner is told more information is needed instead of getting a price.
4. **A text right away.** The homeowner gets a text saying the quote is in their inbox, with your company's details.
5. **An AI call about 5 minutes later.** An AI agent calls the homeowner on your behalf, with their request details, and can book the inspection on your Google Calendar. You get a confirmation text.
6. **Follow-up and reviews.** Leads who don't book get follow-up messages over 28 days. When you mark a job complete, the customer is asked for a Google review.

## What to ask before you choose any service

- **Which leads it covers.** Phone calls, web forms, or both?
- **How it books.** Does it put inspections on your calendar, or send you a message to call back?
- **Whether it quotes.** Can it give a homeowner a price within limits you set?
- **How leads reach you.** What lands in your inbox or CRM, and how fast?
- **Contracts and pricing.** Month-to-month or a term? Per minute, per call, or flat? What happens in your busiest month?
- **Data access.** What does it need access to (phone, email, calendar), and can you disconnect it?
