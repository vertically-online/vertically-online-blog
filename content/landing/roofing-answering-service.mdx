## What a roofing answering service does

A roofing answering service picks up when you can't. When a homeowner calls about a leak or fills in your quote form while you're on a roof, the service responds, gets the job details, and moves them toward an inspection, so they don't call the next roofer on the list.

There are three kinds:

- **Human answering services** put a live receptionist on your line. They're good with unusual calls and upset customers. They usually take a message or follow a script, and they're typically billed by the minute or by the call.
- **AI answering services** answer instantly at any hour, using a voice agent and text messages. The better ones capture job details and book straight onto your calendar.
- **Hybrid services** pair people with AI, for example a human during business hours and AI overflow at night.

The right choice depends on what you need at the end of the call: a message for you to return, or an inspection already on the calendar.

## Why roofers lose jobs to missed calls and slow replies

Roofing leads arrive while you're working. A homeowner fills out your form at 2 PM; you see it at 8 PM and call back the next morning. By then they've often booked whoever got back to them first. You didn't lose that job on price or workmanship. You lost it on time.

Slow replies also cost you hours. Every lead turns into calls, texts, and emails to gather details, price the job, and send a quote, usually in the evening after a full day on a roof.

## What to ask before you choose any answering service

- **Coverage hours.** Is every hour covered, including weekends, holidays, and storm surges?
- **How it books.** Does it put inspections on your calendar, or send you a message to call back?
- **Whether it quotes.** Can it give a homeowner a price within limits you set?
- **How leads reach you.** What lands in your inbox or CRM, and how fast?
- **Contracts and pricing.** Month-to-month or a term? Per minute, per call, or flat? What happens in your busiest month?
- **Data access.** What does it need access to (phone, email, calendar), and can you disconnect it?
