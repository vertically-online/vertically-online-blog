## Why roofers need an answering service

Most roofing leads arrive while you're doing the work: on a roof, in a crawlspace, or driving between jobs. A homeowner with a leak doesn't leave a voicemail and wait. They call the next roofer on the list.

That's the whole problem an answering service solves. The question is what happens after the phone is answered. A message that sits in your inbox until 8 PM doesn't win the job. A booked inspection does.

## What Vertically does when a lead comes in

Vertically handles every new lead the same way, whether it's a phone call or a web form:

1. **It answers right away.** Calls are picked up in your company's name. Web leads get a text and email reply in under 30 seconds, day or night, weekends included.
2. **It gets the job details.** Address, type of work, roof material, urgency, and the homeowner's questions, so you never call back blind.
3. **It books the inspection.** The voice agent schedules the visit straight onto your Google Calendar and the homeowner gets a confirmation.
4. **It keeps going.** Leads who go quiet get automatic follow-up, and finished jobs get a review request.

Every lead also lands in your inbox with the full breakdown: name, address, service type, job details, and any quote that was sent.

## Answering service vs. AI receptionist: what's different

Traditional roofing answering services put a person on the phone. That's a good fit when you mostly need a friendly voice and a message taken. They're typically priced by the minute or by the call, and the receptionist usually can't quote, can't follow up next week, and can't see your price list.

Vertically is built around the outcome you actually want, an inspection on the calendar:

- **Speed:** a reply in seconds, not when an operator frees up.
- **Booking:** inspections go straight onto your Google Calendar.
- **Web leads too:** not just phone calls. Form leads get the same instant response.
- **Quotes:** jobs within the limits you set can get a PDF estimate from your own price list. Bigger jobs come to you for review first.
- **Follow-up:** quiet leads get chased automatically, so a "let me think about it" doesn't turn into a lost job.
