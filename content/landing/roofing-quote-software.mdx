## The quote is where jobs stall

A homeowner asks for a price. Then comes the back-and-forth: calls and texts to gather details, measuring, pricing, revising, and finally sending something over. That takes hours every week, and every day it takes is a day the homeowner can get a quote from someone else.

Most roofing proposal software and roofing estimate software make that process faster for you. Vertically takes it off your plate for the jobs you're happy to quote without a visit.

## How our roofing quote software builds a quote

1. **The homeowner answers a few simple questions** on your website: the type of work, the roof, the property, and how soon they need it.
2. **The price comes from your price list.** During setup we build your pricing and the limits for what can be quoted automatically. It's entirely custom to your company.
3. **A PDF estimate goes out in under a minute**, from your own business email, under your company name.
4. **Bigger jobs come to you first.** Anything outside your limits is held for your review before a price goes out.
5. **Then the inspection gets booked.** An automatic call to the homeowner schedules the visit straight onto your Google Calendar.

You get the full lead breakdown in your inbox the moment it comes in: name, address, service type, job details, and the quote that was sent.

## Roofing proposal software vs. instant quotes: which do you need?

There are two different jobs here, and it helps to be honest about which one you're hiring for.

**Field estimating and proposal tools** help your team measure a roof, build a detailed proposal, and get it signed. If you have salespeople running appointments, you probably want one. They're strongest after the inspection.

**Instant quoting** happens before anyone visits. It answers the first question every homeowner asks ("roughly what will this cost?") at the moment they ask it, which is when they're deciding who to call back. That's what Vertically does, along with the response, follow-up, and booking around it.

## You stay in control

- **Your prices.** Estimates are built from your price list, not an industry average.
- **Your limits.** Only jobs inside the range you choose get an automatic quote.
- **Your review.** Larger and unusual jobs wait for you.
- **Your approval.** We don't launch the quoting without your sign-off.
- **Your name.** Quotes go out from your email address, under your company name.
